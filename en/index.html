    Frequently asked questions

    Good to know

    How long does an installation take?

    In a typical flat, about 3–5 hours. We state the exact duration in your quote in advance.

    How often should an air conditioner be cleaned?

    At least once a year is recommended, and many manufacturers require it as a warranty condition. We will let you know when it is due.

    Is an on-site survey necessary?

    In most cases we can give you an exact quote based on your details and photos. If a survey is needed, we will arrange a short visit.

    Which brands do you work with?

    We install and service all common brands of split and multi-split systems and heat pumps. Just ask us for a quote.

    How can I pay?

    By cash, card or bank transfer. We issue the invoice once the work is completed.
